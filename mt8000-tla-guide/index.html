System overview ภาพรวมระบบ

MT 8000 is Mindray's total laboratory automation (TLA) line. It moves a tube from the moment it is loaded to the moment it is archived, with no hands in between: it checks the tube by camera, centrifuges, removes the cap, delivers the tube to the analyzers, takes it back, recaps it and stores it at 2–8 °C. A middleware (the "dispatch software") decides where each tube goes.

System layout map แผนผังระบบ (ภาพประกอบ ไม่ใช่ผังจริงของแต่ละไซต์)

Pre-analytical

Bulk loader, I/O module, standalone sampling module, centrifuge, decapper. These receive, identify, spin and uncap.

Transport

Narrow 4-track main track with RFID single-tube carriers, plus front track modules that connect directly to each analyzer.

Analytical

Biochemistry and immunoassay (M3 cascades), coagulation, hematology and urinalysis lines connect to the track.

Post-analytical

Decapping & recapping integrated module and refrigerated storage module (0–2 of each) for recapping, QC handling and storage.

How to teach this system in one sentence

"Load anything, anywhere in the right drawer; the cameras decide what the tube is, the middleware decides where it goes, and the cell you fix is always the one whose lamp is yellow or red."

Where does my tube go? A quick decision path

Is it centrifuged?No: use the non-centrifuged drawer. The tube goes through the centrifuge.
Does it have a cap?Yes: it passes the decapper. No: it goes straight to the analyzer.
Is it QC, a calibrator or a micro sample?Use the standalone module with a 21-position rack and adapter.
Does it only need archiving?Use archive-direct (or archive after centrifugation).
Did the camera or LIS reject it?It lands in one of the five abnormal areas. Fix, then reload.

Sample vision window

The camera reports barcode, tube type, height, diameter, cap, centrifugation state, sample and serum volume, and hemolysis, icterus and lipemia (HIL) levels (N, +, ++, +++). The serum index from the image is for reference only; if an analyzer measured the index, the analyzer's result is the one to trust.

Shutting down the main track from the software

Select Main Track, click Shutdown, wait 15 seconds until the belt stops, then press and hold the Power key of the I/O module.

Auto validation and the LIS link การทวนสอบอัตโนมัติ และการเชื่อมต่อ LIS

Principle

The middleware checks each test against the lab's rules. For every test it sends a flag with the result: AV if every rule passed, UV with a failure description if any rule failed. The LIS releases a sample automatically only when all its tests carry AV and no test is missing. Two layers work together: middleware rules plus LIS logic.

Rule categories

  • Result range: the most effective and lowest-pass-rate rule
  • Logic check: related tests must agree (TP above ALB; Tbil not below Dbil)
  • Delta / history: compare with the same patient's earlier results
  • Flags: specific result flags mark normal or abnormal
  • Sample information: gender, age and others must not be blank
  • Sample status: collection time, fibrin, bubbles
  • IQC: QC must be in control. Most customers do not use it, to avoid mass failures after one bad QC.

Four ways to set a range

  • Reference range ± permissible error. Lowest risk and lowest pass rate. Example: Na 137–147 with 4 %: 131–153.
  • Expected pass rate. Use the 5th and 95th percentile of ≥3 months of LIS data (Na 130–155 in the source example).
  • Medical decision level. Needs close work with clinicians.
  • Range by population or disease. Heavy work; fits research more than routine.

Implementation steps

Request→Evaluate risks→Talk to customer→Initial rules→Compare & adjust→Verify→Go live→Re-verify every 6 months
  • Initial rules: import the standard set or build rule by rule, then run them over history (Statistics → Auto-Validation → import history). Tune until each test passes about 80–90 % on history.
  • Compare: doctors validate by hand and compare with the auto result every day, for at least 3 months or 50,000 samples. Record every mismatch (barcode, test, rule, suggestion). The distributor sends records to Mindray at least weekly.
  • Verify before going live: 10 working days or 5,000 samples. Pass criteria: no false negatives and 100 % agreement between manual and automatic validation.
  • Repeat every 6 months, and after any instrument or LIS update or rule change.
Teach this principle: a higher pass rate is not the goal, because it carries more risk. The goal is zero false negatives. A sample that fails validation is not necessarily wrong; it is only unproven.

Risks to explain to the customer

  • Works only for Mindray instruments through the middleware. A sample with non-Mindray tests cannot be fully auto validated.
  • LIS vendors differ. Some cannot display the failure reason; some charge to develop the function.
  • The pass rate cannot be promised. Guidance in the SOP: biochemistry ≈ 60 %, immunoassay target ≈ 80 %.
  • Rules are encrypted inside the middleware and are not given to the LIS without R&D authorisation.
  • The period lasts over 6 months, with clinical application visits at least twice a month.
  • In version EBJ 735, auto validation requires results to be sent by sample, so biochemistry results wait for immunoassay results of the same sample.

LIS protocol (HL7)

Transport

HL7 v2.3.1 over TCP. Messages are framed with MLLP: start byte 0x0B, data (UTF-8), end bytes 0x1C then 0x0D.

Messages

  • ORU^R01 results and QC data (segments MSH, PID, PV1, OBR, OBX, NTE)
  • ACK^R01 acknowledgement
  • ORM^O01 order or worklist query
  • ORR^O02 answer carrying the full order

Auto validation fields

  • OBR-23: sample status (AV passed, UV not passed, else blank)
  • OBX-25: test status (AV, UV, blank)
  • OBX-26: name of the failed rule

Other things the protocol carries

  • Sample volume and hematocrit (OBR 33–35, protocol 2.0)
  • Serum image and sample position data
  • Two-way query with the LIS for orders
  • The LIS need not answer ACK for the sample on-board, sign-off and position-update messages

When a customer asks for LIS work, the sequence is: technical talk with the LIS engineer, send the protocol document, LIS develops and verifies, then customer acceptance. Mindray IT supports online.

Daily operation การใช้งานประจำวัน

Before start

  • Fluidic tubes and cables correctly connected
  • Nothing placed on top of the system
  • Waste container and waste-cap container have room for the day
  • No alarm showing
  • Analyzers checked per their own manuals
  • No tubes left in outlet drawers

Start up

  1. All power switches on.
  2. Press [standby] on the I/O module; all module status lamps come on.
  3. Start the analyzer input/output module ([standby] on its left side) and run its PC software.
  4. Log in to the dispatch software.
  5. Calibration and QC as the analyzers require, then run samples.

Shut down

  1. [standby] on the I/O module.
  2. [standby] on the analyzer input/output module.
  3. Optionally close the dispatch software.
  4. Wait at least 10 seconds before starting again.

Follow the order strictly; the manual warns that wrong shutdown can pollute fluidic tubes and cause wrong results.

Never power off while loading. If the I/O module or track must stop, press Pause on the bulk loader first and power off only after loading has stopped. Otherwise tubes can be carried to a position the system cannot clear by itself.

Daily checks that prevent most calls

  • Empty the waste tube box and press Clear. Discard time for the integrated module is best set between 03:00 and 05:00.
  • Keep the cap hopper filled. An empty hopper raises "Feeder abnormal error".
  • Check the waste bag size: decapper 70×80 cm, integrated module cap bin 80×90, tube bin 50×65.
  • Keep RFID areas of racks dry and remove the transparent rack cover before sliding a rack into a drawer.
  • Label tubes so the barcode is not on the cap and a window is left for the camera.
  • Maintenance in the Operator's Manual is "as needed": wipe covers with water, clean racks with cotton swabs and water or ethanol.
  • Use only validated vacuum tubes (Φ12 or Φ13 × 75 or 100 mm; Φ16×100 listed for BD). Glass tubes are not supported. Push each tube vertically to the rack bottom.
When you train a new user, have them spend ten minutes on the screen colours: blue lamp normal, yellow warning (can keep running, handle soon), red fault (handle now). A yellow drawer lamp means full; flashing yellow means full with samples still waiting to enter the drawer.

Start with the lamp and the screen, then the alarm number. Use "Remove Error" first only when the manual tells you to. If it persists, collect the log and call.

Site survey before installation แบบสำรวจก่อนติดตั้ง TLA

This tab follows your "Survey site form application" and adds what the training files say about each answer. The survey is the input for every setting in this guide: the answers decide which smart functions, rules and drawers you configure. Fill it with the head of the lab, the LIS vendor and the person who runs the instrument.

1. Header and configuration

  • Hospital and survey date. Write the proposal version used (the form shows "MT8000 proposal version 1.742" as an example).
  • System configuration. Count the modules: bulk loader, I/O, decapper, centrifuge, BS-2800 chemistry analyzers, CL-8000i immunoassay analyzers, refrigerator. The post-processing module (0–2 per system) is required for Auto QC.
  • Old system in the lab and current workflow. Standalone, integrated, automation with or without a refrigerator, or automation being replaced. This sets how much training and rule migration is needed.
  • Test items. Routine chemistry plus special items on the form: A1c, hs-CRP, vancomycin (Thermo). Immunoassay items: thyroid, tumor markers, HIV, hepatitis B, cardiac hs-TnI, BNP. Any item that is not Mindray becomes an offline or third-party item and affects sorting and auto validation.
  • HIS and LIS names. The form notes the middleware connection options: check-in, real-time stamp, and whether pictures are sent (yes or no). Ask the LIS vendor now; some LIS vendors cannot show the reason an auto validation failed, and some charge for development.

For each item: what to ask, and where the answer is used. Rows are coloured by topic.

3. Add these questions on site

The form does not list them, but the training files show they cause delays when missed.

  • Barcode and label. Is the barcode ever pasted on the cap? Does the label leave a window for the camera? Thick labels or very tight tubes in the 50-hole rack should be handled on the standalone module.
  • Tubes the line cannot take. Glass tubes are not supported. Capped Greiner tubes in the I/O module give a slip error. Check any 16 mm or special tubes; 16 mm tubes cannot go into the refrigerator.
  • Racks. How many racks are needed for the daily load if the lab wants original-rack archiving, and how many red racks for extra-urgent samples?
  • Result sending. For auto validation in version 735, results are sent by sample. Will the lab accept biochemistry results waiting for immunoassay results of the same sample?
  • Non-Mindray tests. Samples with non-Mindray tests cannot be fully auto validated.
  • Network. Is remote control or a smart screen wanted? A private network needs the hospital IT department.
  • Waste and discard. Waste bag sizes (decapper 70×80 cm, cap bin 80×90, tube bin 50×65), discard time (best between 03:00 and 05:00) and who empties bins daily.
  • Refrigerator capacity. More than 10,000 tubes in a week needs Capacity Optimization (for example send weekend samples to the output area).
  • People. Who is the main operator, who reviews results, who calls Mindray, and what are the shift times? Auto QC and auto validation both depend on a named owner.
